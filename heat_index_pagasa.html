Learning continuity levels

Thresholds and school measures based on the memo image you supplied.

HAYO
LEVEL 01
Below 27°C
MANAGEABLE CONDITIONS

Face-to-face classes may continue with heat-adapted measures as needed. Provide safe drinking water, hydration breaks and ventilated classrooms.

HINTAY
LEVEL 02
27°C–32°C
CAUTION

Consider reduced face-to-face hours, modified schedules, hybrid or home-based learning, and other protective measures as conditions require.

HINGA
LEVEL 03
33°C–41°C
EXTREME CAUTION

Under the supplied memo, suspend face-to-face classes and reduce academic demand. Use light learning tasks, learning packets and well-being check-ins.

HINTO
LEVEL 04
42°C and above
DANGER / EXTREME DANGER

Under the supplied memo, suspend academic activities, including face-to-face and home-based learning. Maintain communication and safety checks.

Heat safety first

Drink water regularly, take breaks in cool or shaded areas, limit strenuous activity during hotter periods and seek prompt help if someone shows signs of heat-related illness.

How to interpret the result

This estimate is not a live PAGASA observation. Actual heat stress also depends on sunshine, wind, exposure, activity and individual circumstances.

Operational disclaimer: The HAYO, HINTAY, HINGA and HINTO labels and school arrangements are based on the supplied memo image. This calculator does not declare class suspensions. School officials should apply the current DepEd policy, local conditions and official PAGASA advisories. The heat-index equation is an estimate and may differ from official values.
